How to listen to web articles on iPhone

You find a long article you want to read but have no time to sit and read it. Here is how to listen to it instead.

Updated November 2026

Free way: Safari

  1. Open the article in Safari.
  2. Open the page menu in the address bar and look for Listen to Page (available on recent iOS versions; the menu layout varies by version).
  3. If you don't see it, use Speak Screen: swipe down from the top with two fingers.

Good for one article at a time. There is no list to queue up several, and adverts and menus on the page can get in the way.

Listen now or later: Reader

  1. Open the web page in Reader, or share it to Reader.
  2. Switch to the simple reader view to strip away clutter, or the column view for a newspaper layout.
  3. Choose Speak now, or Read it later to save it to your list.
  4. Play your list on your commute with the screen locked.
Get Reader on the App Store Free to download, with an optional Premium subscription.

Common questions

Does it work with any website?

Most article pages work well. Pages behind a login or paywall need to be open and readable on your phone first.

Can I listen with the screen off?

Yes. Reader keeps playing in the background, like music.
