How to have a PDF read aloud on iPhone

A 60-page report, a chapter for class, or a long contract. Listening lets you get through it on the bus or while you cook.

Updated November 2026

Free way: Speak Screen

  1. Turn on Speak Screen (Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content).
  2. Open the PDF in Files or Books.
  3. Swipe down from the top with two fingers to start reading.

It works page by page. On long PDFs it can skip, stop, or read headers and page numbers, and you have to keep the PDF open on screen.

Whole document: Reader

  1. In Reader, import the PDF from the Files app.
  2. Tap play. Reader reads through the document, and you can lock the phone.
  3. Search inside the PDF to jump to a part, and change the speed or voice at any time.
  4. Keep documents in folders, and lock private ones with a password.

Printed paper too

Have a book, handout or letter on paper? Scan it with the camera in Reader, or pick a photo of it, and Reader reads the text aloud.

Common questions

Can it read a scanned PDF?

A PDF made of photos of pages has no text for any app to read straight away. Scan the pages in Reader instead, so it can pick up the words.

Will it remember where I stopped?

Keep the document in your Reader list and come back to it. For very long books, a quick search gets you back to the right section.
