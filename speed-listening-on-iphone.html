How to listen faster: speed listening on iPhone

Many people listen to podcasts at 1.5x or 2x. You can do the same with articles and PDFs, and with practice, go faster.

Updated November 2026

What is speed listening?

Speed listening means playing spoken text faster than normal speech. At first, fast speech is hard to follow. After a few days your ear gets used to it, and a speed that felt too fast starts to feel normal. How fast is comfortable differs from person to person, and from easy text to hard text.

How to build up your speed

  1. Start with easy, familiar material, like a news article.
  2. Begin a little faster than normal, around 1.25x to 1.5x.
  3. When it feels comfortable, step up a little. Small steps work better than big jumps.
  4. Slow down again for difficult or important text. Understanding matters more than speed.

Speed listening with Reader

  1. Open a PDF or article in Reader and press play.
  2. Raise the speech rate. Reader goes up to 8x, far beyond what most people need, so there is always room to grow.
  3. Try a few voices. Some voices stay clearer than others at high speed.

Common questions

Can the built-in iPhone voice go fast?

You can change the speaking rate in Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, but the range is limited compared with a dedicated app.

Is faster always better?

No. Pick the fastest speed at which you still understand and remember what you hear.
